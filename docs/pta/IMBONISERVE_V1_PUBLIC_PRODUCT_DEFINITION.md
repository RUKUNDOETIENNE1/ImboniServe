## The Answer

### ImboniServe Version 1.0 — What a Restaurant Receives on Day One

When a restaurant signs up for ImboniServe, they receive a **complete restaurant operations platform** accessible from any device via web browser or installable PWA. The platform is ready to use immediately after a 2-minute signup process.

---

### What They Get

**Operations**
- A real-time dashboard showing every sale, table status, and order as it happens
- A unified orders feed tracking dine-in, takeaway, and delivery in one place
- A kitchen display system that routes orders to the right stations in real-time
- Table management with sections, occupancy tracking, and QR code assignment
- A reservation system for managing bookings with confirmation and no-show tracking
- A mobile-optimized waiter station for order taking at the table
- A service replay tool for reviewing operational performance

**Menu & Inventory**
- Full menu management with categories, prices, photos, descriptions, and availability toggles
- Inventory tracking with stock levels, unit costs, movement audit trails, and configurable reorder levels
- Four-level inventory alerts (CRITICAL, HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW) sent via WhatsApp when stock crosses thresholds
- OCR document processing for supplier invoices and inventory documents
- AI-powered auto-reorder with automatic draft purchase order generation grouped by supplier (Premium plan)

**QR & Digital**
- A QR code builder that generates branded QR codes for each table
- QR analytics showing scan counts, orders per QR, and revenue attribution by table

**Reports**
- Daily, weekly, and monthly reports covering revenue, costs, and margins
- End-of-day close day process with Z-Report generation and reconciliation
- Menu performance analytics showing item popularity and profitability
- Peak hours analytics for staffing and capacity planning
- Payment analytics with method breakdown and success rates

**Team**
- Staff management with role assignment and performance tracking
- Role-based access control with 9 roles: Owner, Admin, Manager, Chef, Kitchen Staff, Waiter, Cashier, Supervisor, Front Desk

**Payments**
- Mobile money payments: MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, and IremboPay with 20-minute payment timeout
- Cash payment tracking
- Transaction history with status tracking
- Payout summary with fee breakdown
- Configurable payment settings per provider

**Customer Experience**
- Smart Dining Slips™ — auto-generated digital receipts with shareable links
- WhatsApp notifications for order alerts, daily summaries, and low-stock warnings
- Multi-branch control for managing multiple locations from one dashboard (Business plan and above)

**Growth**
- A customer referral program with code generation, sharing tools, and a leaderboard
- A discovery listing on the public ImboniServe directory
- Access to the Founding Restaurant Program (50% lifetime discount for the first 100 restaurants)

**Platform**
- Installable Progressive Web App (works on any phone, tablet, or computer)
- 5 pricing plans starting at 15,000 RWF/month with a 14-day free trial
- Multi-language support: English, French, and Kinyarwanda
- Light and dark mode
- Standard or priority support depending on plan

---

### What They Do NOT Get

The following features are **not available** in Version 1.0 and are **not marketed** as available:

- Website builder
- Hotel mode
- AI menu builder (photo-to-menu)
- WhatsApp marketing campaigns
- Menu A/B testing
- Voice ordering via WhatsApp AI
- Reservation deposits
- Card / POS terminal payments
- CRM with automated campaigns (available as Early Access by request)
- Loyalty program (available as Early Access by request)
- Promotions engine (available as Early Access by request)
- Supplier marketplace (Coming Soon)
- API access
- White-label options
- Custom integrations
- SSO

---

### What They Need to Do to Get Started

1. **Sign up** — Create an account at imboniserve.com (2 minutes)
2. **Build menu** — Add dishes, drinks, prices, and photos
3. **Set up tables** — Create tables and generate QR codes
4. **Connect WhatsApp** — Link a WhatsApp number for notifications
5. **Enable payments** — Configure MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, or IremboPay
6. **Add inventory** — Add stock items with reorder levels and minimum thresholds
7. **Go live** — Print QR codes, place on tables, start accepting orders
