# Founder Success Portal — Onboarding Guide

## Welcome to the Founder Success Portal

This guide walks you through your first 30 minutes in the portal, helping you get set up and start growing your partnership business.

---

## Step 1: Log In (2 minutes)

1. Navigate to `/portal` in your browser
2. Enter your partnership account email and password
3. You'll arrive at the Home page — your daily workspace

---

## Step 2: Complete Your Profile (3 minutes)

1. Click **Profile** in the sidebar
2. Fill in your **Phone**, **Organization**, and **Region**
3. Click **Save Changes**
4. Review your agreement details on the same page

---

## Step 3: Review Your Snapshot (3 minutes)

On the **Home** page:
- Check your **Active Trials** and **Paying Businesses** counts
- Note your **This Month's Commission** and the trend arrow
- Read the **Growth Coach** recommendations — these are prioritized for you

---

## Step 4: Check Your Founder Codes (5 minutes)

1. Click **My Founder Codes** in the sidebar
2. Review your existing codes and their performance
3. If you have an active code, click **Copy** to get the code
4. Click **Share** to share it via social media or WhatsApp
5. Note the **conversion rate** — aim to improve it over time

---

## Step 5: Create Your First Campaign (5 minutes)

1. Click **My Campaigns** in the sidebar
2. Click **New Campaign**
3. Fill in:
   - **Name**: e.g., "August Restaurant Outreach"
   - **Channel**: e.g., WhatsApp, In-Person, Social Media
   - **Target Signups**: e.g., 10
   - **Target Conversions**: e.g., 3
4. Click **Create**
5. Your campaign is now active and will track signups and conversions

---

## Step 6: Explore Learning Resources (5 minutes)

1. Click **Learning Center** in the sidebar
2. Read "How to Recruit Restaurants" (5 min read)
3. Read "Best Practices for Follow-Up" (7 min read)
4. Check the FAQs for common questions

---

## Step 7: Download Marketing Materials (3 minutes)

1. Click **Marketing Resources** in the sidebar
2. Download the **Brand Guide** to understand how to present ImboniServe
3. Download **WhatsApp Status Template** for social media posting
4. Download **WhatsApp Outreach Template** for messaging restaurant owners

---

## Step 8: Understand Your Earnings (4 minutes)

1. Click **My Earnings** in the sidebar
2. Review:
   - **Current Month** commission
   - **Pending** commissions (awaiting validation)
   - **Approved** commissions (ready for payout)
   - **Upcoming Payout** amount
3. Click through **Payment History** to see past payouts

---

## Your First Week Checklist

- [ ] Profile completed
- [ ] At least one Founder Code shared
- [ ] First campaign created
- [ ] First article read
- [ ] Marketing materials downloaded
- [ ] Earnings page reviewed

---

## Tips for Success

1. **Check the Home page daily** — the Growth Coach updates with new recommendations
2. **Follow up on trials ending soon** — the Home page alerts you 3 days before trials expire
3. **Track your Growth page weekly** — monitor month-over-month trends
4. **Create targeted campaigns** — different campaigns for different restaurant types
5. **Use message templates** — they're proven to work
6. **Submit support tickets early** — don't wait if you have questions

---

## Need Help?

- Click **Support** in the sidebar to submit a help request
- Check **Messages** for announcements and updates
- Review the **Learning Center** FAQs
