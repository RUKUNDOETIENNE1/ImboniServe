## Operational Readiness Statement

The Executive Operating System is **READY** to support real executive decision-making.

An executive leadership team using this system can:
1. See company-wide health at a glance (Intelligence Pulse)
2. Identify which center needs attention (Center Health Radar)
3. Understand what changed and why (Trend Explanations)
4. Know what to prioritize today (Priority Queue)
5. Make informed decisions with cross-center evidence (Executive Decisions)
6. Assess risks and mitigations (Business Risks)
7. Identify growth opportunities (Growth Opportunities)
8. Drill down from any metric to the operational workspace
9. Navigate between centers seamlessly
10. Trust that all metrics are reconciled across centers

The system is not a dashboard — it is a decision intelligence environment that enables strategic, financial, operational, growth, partnership, and customer success decision-making through one coherent architecture.
