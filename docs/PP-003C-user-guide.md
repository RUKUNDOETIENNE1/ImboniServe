# Founder Success Portal — User Guide

## Getting Started

### Accessing the Portal
1. Go to `/portal` in your browser
2. Log in with your partnership account credentials
3. You will be redirected to the Home page

### Navigation
The portal sidebar provides access to all sections:
- **Home** — Your daily overview
- **My Growth** — Track your performance trends
- **My Campaigns** — Manage marketing campaigns
- **My Founder Codes** — View and share referral codes
- **My Businesses** — See businesses you've referred
- **My Earnings** — Track commission and payouts
- **Learning Center** — Articles and FAQs
- **Marketing Resources** — Download branded assets
- **Messages** — Announcements and updates
- **Support** — Submit help requests
- **Profile** — Manage your details

---

## Home Page

The Home page gives you a quick snapshot of how you're doing today:
- **Welcome card** shows your name, active trials, paying businesses, and this month's commission
- **Success Snapshot** displays key metrics: active trials, paying businesses, total referrals, lifetime commission, and this month's commission with trend
- **Growth Coach** provides personalized recommendations to improve your performance
- **Milestones** celebrates your achievements and shows progress toward your next goals
- **Recent Activity** shows your latest actions and events

---

## My Growth

Track your month-over-month performance:
- Compare signups, conversions, and commission vs last month
- View a 6-month trend chart
- See growth opportunities surfaced by the system

---

## My Campaigns

Create and manage marketing campaigns:
- **Create** a new campaign with target signups and conversions
- **Pause** an active campaign temporarily
- **Resume** a paused campaign
- **Duplicate** a campaign to reuse its settings
- **Archive** a campaign when done
- Filter campaigns by status (All, Active, Paused, Draft, Completed, Cancelled)

---

## My Founder Codes

View and share your referral codes:
- See each code's performance: businesses, active trials, subscribers, revenue, conversion rate
- **Copy** a code to your clipboard
- **Share** a code via social media or messaging
- **QR Code** for offline sharing
- See which campaign each code belongs to

---

## My Businesses

View all businesses you've referred:
- See business name, city, type, and status
- Track trial start/end dates
- See which have active subscriptions
- Identify businesses that need follow-up

---

## My Earnings

Understand your commission in detail:
- **Current Month** commission and count
- **Lifetime** total earnings
- **Pending** commissions (awaiting validation)
- **Validated** commissions (confirmed but not yet approved)
- **Approved** commissions (ready for payout)
- **Paid** commissions (already withdrawn)
- **Upcoming Payout** amount
- **Payment History** with dates and methods

---

## Learning Center

Access articles to help you grow:
- Categories: Getting Started, Sales, Marketing, Finance, Industry
- FAQs for common questions

---

## Marketing Resources

Download branded materials:
- **Brand Assets** — Logo, brand guide
- **Social Media Templates** — Instagram, WhatsApp
- **Print Materials** — QR posters, flyers
- **Message Templates** — Email and WhatsApp outreach scripts

---

## Messages

Stay informed with:
- Announcements and product updates
- Campaign suggestions
- Performance milestone notifications
- Support replies
- Finance messages
- Filter by message type

---

## Support

Get help when you need it:
- Submit a new support request with subject, category, and message
- Track your ticket history and status (Open, In Progress, Resolved, Closed)

---

## Profile

Manage your account:
- View your partner type and join date
- Edit your name, phone, organization, and region
- View your current agreement details
