## Communication Guidelines

### Tone
- **Warm but professional:** You are a hospitality technology partner, not a cold SaaS vendor
- **Concise:** Restaurant owners are busy. Get to the point quickly.
- **Honest:** If something is broken, say so. If you don't know, say so.
- **Encouraging:** Celebrate milestones. Acknowledge progress.

### Language
- **Primary:** English (standard for Rwandan business communication)
- **Secondary:** Kinyarwanda (available for informal WhatsApp communication if preferred by customer)
- **French:** Available if customer prefers

### Timing
- **Send emails:** Morning (8:00-10:00 AM) for best open rates
- **Send WhatsApp:** Anytime during business hours (7:00 AM - 9:00 PM)
- **Schedule calls:** Afternoon (2:00-5:00 PM) when restaurant owners are less busy

### Personalization
- Always use the customer's name
- Always reference their business name
- Include specific metrics from their dashboard when relevant
- Reference previous conversations to show continuity
