# OEC-001E Executive Workflow Assessment

## From Insight to Action: Do Executive Workflows Naturally Lead to Execution?

---

## 1. Executive Daily Brief

### Implementation

Every executive center has a Daily Brief component:
- CEO: `DailyBrief.tsx` — Yesterday revenue, today priorities, risks, opportunities
- CFO: `FinancialDailyBrief.tsx` — Yesterday collections, forecast, liabilities
- COO: `CooDailyBrief.tsx` — Yesterday operations, today workload, achievements
- CMO: `CmoDailyBrief.tsx` — Yesterday growth, today opportunities, conversion trends
- Partnership: `PartnershipDailyBrief.tsx` — Yesterday partners, today priorities, renewals
- CS Director: `CustomerSuccessDailyBrief.tsx` — Yesterday customers, today priorities, activations
- Executive Intelligence: `IntelligencePulse.tsx` — Overall health, center radar

### Assessment

| Criterion | Status | Notes |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| Yesterday summary | ✅ | All centers show yesterday metrics |
| Today priorities | ✅ | All centers show today's priorities |
| Risks | ✅ | All centers identify risks |
| Opportunities | ✅ | All centers identify opportunities |
| Activity tracking | ✅ | All centers track relevant activity |

**Score: 5/5 — Excellent**

## 3. Executive Workflows

### Insight → Action Flow

| Center | Insight | Action | Verification |
|--------|---------|--------|--------------|
| CEO | Focus Card alert | Click alert → operational page | ❌ No return/verification |
| CFO | Integrity score drop | Click → reconciliation page | ❌ No return/verification |
| COO | Support queue spike | Click → support center | ❌ No return/verification |
| CMO | Conversion rate drop | Click → founder partners | ❌ No return/verification |
| Partnership | Expiring agreement | Click → partner management | ❌ No return/verification |
| CS Director | At-risk customer | Click → restaurants | ❌ No return/verification |
| Exec Intel | Cross-center risk | Click → relevant center | ❌ No return/verification |

### Assessment

| Criterion | Status | Notes |
|-----------|--------|-------|
| Insight → Action | ✅ | All centers have drill-down |
| Action → Verification | ❌ | No closed-loop tracking |
| Context preservation | ❌ | No breadcrumb/state preservation |

**Score: 3/5 — Moderate** (Insight → Action works; Action → Verification missing)

## 6. Decision Speed

### Time to Key Information

| Information | Time | How |
|-------------|------|-----|
| What's happening? | <30s | Focus/Pulse card immediately visible |
| Critical alerts | <30s | Top of Focus card, red color |
| Health score | <30s | Prominent display in Focus/Pulse |
| Today's priorities | <1min | Daily Brief section |
| AI recommendations | <2min | AI Assistant section (scroll) |
| Drill-down to details | <5s | Click KPI card or attention item |

| Criterion | Status | Notes |
