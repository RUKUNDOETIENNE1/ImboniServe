# EOS-001G — Customer Success Director Operating Center User Guide

## Overview

The Customer Success Director Operating Center is the Customer Success Director's daily command center for ensuring that every Hospitality Business becomes successful after joining ImboniServe. It is accessible at `/admin/executive/customer-success-director` and requires the CUSTOMER_SUCCESS_DIRECTOR, ADMIN, CUSTOMER_SUCCESS_MANAGER, or EXECUTIVE role.

This is not a support dashboard. This is not a ticket management page. It is the executive operating environment from which the Customer Success Director manages activation, adoption, usage, engagement, health, support, satisfaction, expansion, renewals, retention, and advocacy.

Every section answers one question: "Which Hospitality Businesses are succeeding, which are at risk, and what actions should we take today to maximize long-term customer success?"

---

## Getting Started

1. Navigate to **Customer Success Center** in the admin sidebar
2. The page loads automatically with current customer success intelligence
3. Click **Refresh** to update data at any time

---

## Sections

### 1. Customer Success Pulse
Your signature overview. Shows:
- **Customer Success Health Score** (0-100) — composite of activation rate, retention, adoption, engagement, and support burden
- **Active Hospitality Businesses** — currently active businesses
- **New Activations** — businesses activated in last 7 days
- **Businesses at Risk** — businesses with low or no recent activity
- **Healthy Businesses** — active businesses without risk indicators
- **Retention Rate** — active subscriptions as % of total
- **Expansion Opportunities** — businesses ready for expansion
- **Success Status** — HEALTHY / WARNING / CRITICAL
- **Today's Summary** — narrative overview of customer success state

Click any KPI card to drill into the relevant workspace.

### 2. Customer Success Daily Brief
Your morning briefing with:
- **Yesterday** — active businesses, active customers, sales activity
- **Today's Priorities** — expiring trials, grace period subscriptions, high-priority support
- **New Activations** — new businesses and activations
- **Customers Requiring Attention** — low activity, no activity, dormant customers
- **Success Highlights** — recent wins and positive trends
- **Retention Risks** — grace period, past due, cancellations, inactive businesses
- **Recommendations** — AI-suggested actions

Click the header to collapse/expand.

### 3. Customer Journey Intelligence
Visualize the complete customer lifecycle:
- Lead → Trial → Activation → Onboarding → Adoption → Healthy Customer → Expansion → Advocate
- **Bottleneck indicator** — highlights the stage with the highest count
- **Stage metrics** — In Trial, Adopted, Healthy, Expansion Ready counts
- Click any stage to drill into the relevant workspace

### 4. Customer Health Center
Monitor customer health across the platform:
- **Overall Health Score** (0-100) — composite customer success health
- **Health Distribution** — Excellent / Healthy / At Risk / Critical segments
- **High-Risk Businesses** — businesses with low or no activity
- **Improving Businesses** — recently activated businesses
- **Declining Businesses** — businesses with no recent activity
- **Health Trends** — customer activity, adoption, retention, dormant customers with trend indicators
- **Health Drivers** — key metrics driving the health score

Click any metric to drill into the relevant workspace.

### 5. Adoption Intelligence
Track platform adoption and feature usage:
- **Adoption Rate** — businesses with recent sales as % of total
- **Active Branches** — active branches out of total
- **Active Users (7d)** — business users active in last 7 days
- **Sales (7d)** — total sales in last 7 days
- **Feature Adoption** — QR In-Venue Ordering and Remote Ordering enablement counts
- **Usage Activity** — active users (7d/30d), active customers (7d)
- **Underutilized Features** — features with low adoption and drill-down links

Click any metric to drill into the relevant workspace.

### 6. Customer Engagement Center
Monitor customer engagement across the platform:
- **Total Customers** — all end-consumers across the platform
- **Active (30d)** — customers active in last 30 days with active rate
- **New (7d)** — new customers in last 7 days
- **Dormant (90d+)** — customers with no visit in 90+ days
- **Platform Engagement** — active users, active customers
- **Support Interactions** — open conversations, high-priority items
- **Recent Support Conversations** — latest open conversations with priority badges

Click any metric to drill into the relevant workspace.

### 7. Retention & Expansion Center
Manage retention and identify expansion opportunities:
- **Retention Rate** — active subscriptions as % of total
- **Churn Rate** — cancelled subscriptions as % of total
- **Active Subscriptions** — currently active subscriptions
- **Renewals (30d)** — subscriptions renewing in next 30 days
- **Renewal Risk** — grace period, past due, cancelled subscriptions
- **Expansion Opportunities** — businesses with active branches and engaged customers
- **Upcoming Renewals** — subscriptions renewing soon with amount and days remaining

Click any metric to drill into the relevant workspace.

### 8. Success Opportunity Center
Automatically identified growth opportunities:
- **EXPANSION** — businesses ready for upsell or multi-branch expansion
- **TRIAL_CONVERSION** — trials expiring soon, ready for conversion
- **ADOPTION_IMPROVEMENT** — low-activity businesses needing training
- **RE_ENGAGEMENT** — businesses with no activity needing outreach
- **FEATURE_ADOPTION** — businesses that could benefit from QR/remote ordering
- **CUSTOMER_RE_ENGAGEMENT** — dormant customers across the platform
- **REGIONAL_EXPANSION** — underpenetrated cities
- **SUCCESS_MILESTONE** — highly active businesses ready for advocacy
- Every opportunity includes a description, action, and expected impact

Click any opportunity to drill into the relevant workspace.

### 9. Customer Attention Center
Actionable items only — no informational cards:
- Sorted by severity (CRITICAL → HIGH → MEDIUM → LOW)
- Each item includes title, description, and specific action
- Sources: expiring trials, grace period, past due, low/no activity, dormant customers, high-priority support, open support, renewals, cancellations, inactive businesses
- Green status when no items need attention

Click any item to drill into the relevant workspace.

### 10. AI Customer Success Assistant
Deterministic, rule-based recommendations:
- Each recommendation includes:
  - **Question** — what customer success decision to address
  - **Answer** — data-driven response
  - **Evidence** — supporting data points
  - **Confidence** — percentage confidence level (color-coded bar)
  - **Expected Impact** — anticipated outcome
  - **Suggested Actions** — clickable action buttons
- Recommendations are never fabricated — all based on real data
- 7 recommendation types: ecosystem health, trial conversion, retention/churn, adoption/engagement, customer engagement, expansion, support health

## Drill-Down Navigation

Every KPI and metric links to an existing workspace:
- **Hospitality Businesses** — `/admin/restaurants`
- **Subscriptions** — `/admin/subscriptions`
- **Operations Intelligence** — `/admin/operations-intelligence`
- **Users** — `/admin/users`

No dead-end metrics.

## Tips

- Start with the **Customer Success Pulse** to get a 30-second overview of ecosystem health
- Check **Customer Attention Center** for urgent items requiring action
- Review **Success Opportunity Center** for growth and expansion actions
- Use **AI Customer Success Assistant** for strategic customer success decisions
- Monitor **Customer Journey Intelligence** for pipeline bottlenecks
- Check **Retention & Expansion Center** for upcoming renewals and churn risk
- Use **Adoption Intelligence** to identify underutilized features and training needs
- Refresh data before making decisions
