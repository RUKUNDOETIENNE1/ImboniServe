# EOS-001E — CMO Operating Center User Guide

## Overview

The CMO Operating Center is the Chief Marketing Officer's daily workspace for managing ImboniServe's growth engine. It is accessible at `/admin/executive/cmo` and requires the CMO, ADMIN, or EXECUTIVE role.

---

## Getting Started

1. Navigate to **CMO Command Center** in the admin sidebar
2. The page loads automatically with current growth intelligence
3. Click **Refresh** to update data at any time

---

## Sections

### 1. Growth Pulse
Your signature overview. Shows:
- **Overall Growth Score** (0-100) — composite health metric
- **Restaurant Growth** — new businesses in last 7 days
- **Founder Growth** — new partners in last 7 days
- **Campaign Momentum** — active vs draft vs paused campaigns
- **Conversion Rate** — overall funnel conversion
- **Regional Expansion** — number of active regions
- **Acquisition Trend** — ACCELERATING / STABLE / DECLINING
- **Marketing Health** — overall status
- **Activation Rate** — active businesses as % of total

Click any KPI card to drill into the relevant workspace.

### 2. CMO Daily Brief
Your morning briefing with:
- **Yesterday** — key metrics from the previous day
- **Today's Opportunities** — auto-detected growth opportunities
- **Growth Achievements** — recent wins
- **Campaign Highlights** — top-performing campaigns
- **Conversion Trends** — funnel conversion rates
- **Risks** — items requiring attention
- **Recommendations** — AI-suggested actions
- **Upcoming Launches** — draft campaigns ready to go

### 3. Campaign Performance Center
Track all campaigns:
- Active, draft, paused, and completed counts
- Top-performing campaigns with conversion rates, signups, revenue
- Channel performance breakdown
- Click any campaign to drill into Growth Workspace

### 4. Acquisition Funnel
Visualize the complete acquisition journey:
- Visitor → Lead → Interested Restaurant → Trial → Activation → Subscription → Retained Customer
- Conversion rates at each stage
- Drop-off counts and percentages
- Click any stage to drill into the relevant workspace

### 5. Founder Marketing Network
Track founder partner performance:
- Code statistics (total, active, expired, redemptions)
- Top partners by signups, conversions, and revenue
- Partner health scores with grades
- Click any partner to drill into Founder Portal

### 6. Regional Growth Intelligence
Geographic growth analysis:
- Growth by region (signups, conversions, revenue)
- Restaurant density by city
- Untapped regions with low acquisition
- Click any region to drill into Operations Intelligence

### 7. Marketing Opportunity Center
Automatically identified opportunities:
- **SCALE** — high-performing campaigns ready to expand
- **LAUNCH** — draft campaigns ready to activate
- **OPTIMIZE** — underperforming campaigns to review
- **EXPAND** — untapped regions for growth
- **SUPPORT** — re-engagement opportunities
- Every opportunity includes a recommended action and expected impact

### 8. Brand & Engagement Overview
Platform engagement metrics:
- QR code adoption and scan statistics
- Referral link activity (clicks, signups)
- Business invite conversion rates
- Platform usage (active businesses, subscriptions, trials)
- Acquisition source attribution breakdown

### 9. Marketing Attention Center
Actionable items only — no informational cards:
- Sorted by severity (CRITICAL → LOW)
- Each item includes a specific action and drill-down link
- Green status when no items need attention

### 10. AI Marketing Assistant
Deterministic, rule-based recommendations:
- Each recommendation includes:
  - **Question** — what marketing decision to address
  - **Answer** — data-driven response
  - **Evidence** — supporting data points
  - **Confidence** — percentage confidence level
  - **Expected Impact** — anticipated outcome
  - **Suggested Actions** — clickable action buttons
- Recommendations are never fabricated — all based on real data

## Drill-Down Navigation

Every KPI and metric links to an existing workspace:
- **Growth Workspace** — `/admin/founder-partners`
- **Founder Portal** — `/admin/founder-partners`
- **Applications** — `/admin/partnership-applications`
- **Revenue Operations** — `/admin/revenue-operations`
- **Operations Intelligence** — `/admin/operations-intelligence`
- **Restaurants** — `/admin/restaurants`
- **Subscriptions** — `/admin/subscriptions`

---

## Tips

- Start with the **Growth Pulse** to get a 30-second overview
- Check **Marketing Attention Center** for urgent items
- Review **Marketing Opportunity Center** for growth actions
- Use **AI Marketing Assistant** for investment decisions
- Refresh data before making decisions
