# EOS-001D — COO Operating Center User Guide

## Overview

The COO Operating Center is your daily operational workspace. It is designed to help you ensure operational excellence and efficient execution across the platform. This is not a dashboard — it is an action-oriented environment.

## Access

- **URL**: `/admin/executive/coo`
- **Roles**: COO, ADMIN, OPERATIONS_MANAGER, EXECUTIVE
- **Navigation**: Click "COO Command Center" in the admin sidebar (Activity icon)

## Sections

### 1. Operations Pulse

Your at-a-glance operational health summary. Shows:
- **Operations Score** (0-100): Weighted score based on payment, queue, reconciliation, subscription health, and operational backlog.
- **Key metrics**: Platform health, restaurants waiting, founder activations pending, support queue, critical incidents, average response time, operational capacity, payment health.
- **Click any metric** to drill down to the underlying workflow.

### 2. COO Daily Brief

Your daily summary of what happened and what needs to happen:
- **Yesterday**: Revenue, change, new businesses.
- **Today's Workload**: Pending applications, open support, pending payouts.
- **Achievements**: Items completed yesterday.
- **Pending Work**: Items still needing attention.
- **Risks**: Critical and high-severity items.
- **Escalations**: Suspended partners and other escalations.
- **Recommendations**: AI-suggested actions.
- **Resource Constraints**: Staffing and capacity issues.

### 3. Operational Health Center

Health status across 7 operational areas:
- Platform, Restaurant Operations, Founder Operations, Support, Payments, Revenue Operations, Internal Teams.
- Each area shows health status (HEALTHY/WARNING/CRITICAL), trend, and risk description.
- **Click any area** to drill down.

### 4. Restaurant Operations

Operational view of all businesses:
- Active/inactive counts, new yesterday, activation rate.
- Follow-up alerts for businesses needing attention (10+ days without action).
- Regional distribution of signups and conversions.
- **Click "View All Businesses"** for the full restaurant list.

### 5. Founder Operations

Complete view of the founder partner pipeline:
- **Application Pipeline**: Pending, under review, approved, rejected.
- **Activation Pipeline**: Applied, onboarded, active, suspended.
- **Operational Delays**: Alerts when applications are backing up.
- **Partner Health**: Top 10 partners by health score with grade.
- **Click "View Founder Partners"** for the full partner list.

### 6. Support Operations

Support ticket operations:
- Open, pending, resolved counts.
- High-priority items and SLA compliance.
- Unassigned alerts when conversations lack staff assignment.
- Workload distribution (assigned vs unassigned vs resolved yesterday).
- **Click "View Support Center"** for the full support queue.

### 7. Workflow Performance

6 key operational workflows with duration tracking:
- Application → Approval
- Approval → Activation
- Activation → Campaign
- Restaurant Signup → Active
- Trial → Subscription
- Commission → Payout

Each shows current duration, target duration, trend (ON_TRACK/SLOW), and bottleneck info. **Click any workflow** to drill down.

### 8. Capacity Center

Current operational capacity:
- Support workload, pending approvals, open investigations, daily throughput.
- Expansion readiness indicator: Tells you if operations are efficient enough to scale.

### 9. Operational Attention Center

Severity-sorted list of items requiring your attention:
- CRITICAL items first, then HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW.
- Each item has a description, recommended action, and drill-down link.
- When no items need attention, shows a green "all systems running smoothly" message.

### 10. AI Operations Assistant

Evidence-based operational recommendations:
- Each recommendation answers a key operational question.
- Includes evidence (data points supporting the recommendation).
- Confidence score (0-100%) with visual bar.
- Suggested actions with clickable drill-down.

## Tips

- **Refresh**: Click the Refresh button to reload all data.
- **Drill down**: Click any metric, health area, workflow, or attention item to navigate to the underlying workflow.
- **Expand/collapse**: The Operations Pulse and Daily Brief sections can be collapsed for more screen space.
