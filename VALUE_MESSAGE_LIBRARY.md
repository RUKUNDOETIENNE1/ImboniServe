## 2. Capability Pillar Messages

### Pillar 1: Order Anywhere
**Headline:** "Order Anywhere. Serve Everywhere."
**Sub-headline:** QR, tablet, POS, pre-order, pickup — one menu, every channel.

**Feature messages:**
- **QR Ordering:** "Customers scan, browse your menu, and order directly from their phones — no app needed."
- **Tablet Ordering:** "Prefer tablets? Your staff can take orders at the table with the same menu and real-time kitchen sync."
- **POS:** "Ring up sales at the counter with full inventory deduction and payment processing."
- **Pre-Order & Pickup:** "Accept advance orders for pickup. Customers order ahead, you prepare on time."
- **QR Builder:** "Design branded QR codes with your colors, logo, and message. Generate for every table in seconds."

**Proof point:** "One menu powers every ordering channel — changes sync instantly across QR, tablet, and POS."

### Pillar 2: Kitchen Intelligence
**Headline:** "From Ticket to Table — Without the Chaos."
**Sub-headline:** Real-time KDS, station routing, waiter workflow, and customer messaging.

**Feature messages:**
- **Kitchen Display System (KDS):** "Orders flow directly to the kitchen with status tracking: Pending → Accepted → Preparing → Almost Ready → Ready → Served."
- **Station Routing:** "Route items to the right station — bar, grill, cold prep — automatically."
- **Waiter Dashboard:** "Waiters see what's ready for pickup, what's being prepared, and what's delayed — all in real-time."
- **Customer Messaging:** "Send status updates to customers: 'Please wait,' 'Item unavailable,' 'Almost ready,' 'Ready for pickup.'"
- **Time Tracking:** "Every order is timed. Urgent alerts highlight orders exceeding your service threshold."
- **KDS Advanced:** "Course firing and expo mode for fine dining and high-volume kitchens."

**Proof point:** "Kitchen status transitions are enforced — no skipped steps, no lost tickets, no confusion."

### Pillar 3: Total Inventory Control
**Headline:** "Every Ingredient. Every Cost. Every Reorder — Automated."
**Sub-headline:** Track stock, manage recipes, and let AI handle reordering.

**Feature messages:**
- **Inventory Tracking:** "Every stock movement is recorded with who, what, and when — complete accountability."
- **Recipe Management:** "Link ingredients to menu items. Food costs are calculated from actual consumption, not estimates."
- **Low-Stock Alerts:** "Get push notifications before inventory drops below reorder points."
- **AI Auto-Reorder:** "AI predicts stockouts based on your sales patterns and generates draft purchase orders — approve with one click."
- **Supplier Orders:** "Create and track purchase orders. Confirm deliveries. Keep costs under control."
- **Procurement Workflow:** "From reorder suggestion to approved PO to delivered stock — one workflow."

**Proof point:** "Food costs calculated from actual ingredient consumption — not industry averages or guesses."

### Pillar 4: Financial Command Center
**Headline:** "CFO-Grade Financials. Without the CFO."
**Sub-headline:** Revenue intelligence, payment monitoring, payout reconciliation, and multi-currency support.

**Feature messages:**
- **CFO Dashboard:** "Financial intelligence dashboard with revenue trends, cost analysis, and margin tracking across branches."
- **CEO Dashboard:** "Executive overview with KPIs, growth metrics, and business health indicators at a glance."
- **Payment Monitor:** "Watch payments process in real-time. Catch failures instantly. Confirm transactions as they complete."
- **Payment Analytics:** "Break down payments by method, track success rates, and see fee savings — MTN MoMo, Airtel Money, Card, Cash."
- **Payout Summary:** "See gross revenue, platform commission, and net payout for any period — down to individual sales."
- **Z-Report / Close Day:** "End-of-day reconciliation with payment breakdowns, order sources, VAT, and reservation summary."
- **Multi-Currency:** "Configure your currency. Support cross-border operations with flexible currency settings."
- **Transactions Ledger:** "Every transaction recorded and searchable — full audit trail for accounting."

**Proof point:** "From the first order to the Z-Report, every franc is tracked, categorized, and reconciled."
