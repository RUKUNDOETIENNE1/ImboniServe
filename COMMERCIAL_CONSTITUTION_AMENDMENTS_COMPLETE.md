## AMENDMENTS COMPLETED

### ✅ Amendment 1: Product Demonstration Principle

**Section Added:** Section 1.5  
**Founder Decision:** Decision #3 (Guided Professional Trial) — Product Demonstration component

**What Changed:**
- Added new constitutional section distinguishing demonstrations from commercial entitlements
- Established that demonstrations are educational, not contractual
- Clarified that showing a capability in a demo does not imply subscription inclusion

**Impact:** Prevents confusion about customer entitlements based on sales demonstrations

---

### ✅ Amendment 2: "Only Sell What Exists" Philosophy

**Section Modified:** Principle 2 (Section 1.2)  
**Founder Decision:** Decision #2 (Only Sell What Exists)

**What Changed:**
- Strengthened "Promise = Reality" principle
- Explicitly stated: "Pricing page is a commercial contract, not a product roadmap"
- Established clear rules: Production-ready only, "Early Access" allowed, remove if not operational
- Added "Roadmap as Pricing" to anti-patterns

**Impact:** Enforces Commercial Truth by prohibiting advertising of future capabilities in pricing

---

### ✅ Amendment 3: Customer Growth Philosophy

**Section Modified:** Principle 5 (Section 1.2)  
**Founder Decision:** Decision #1 (Progressive Commercial Discovery)

**What Changed:**
- Added: "Customers should never feel restricted—they should feel that the platform grows together with their business"
- Shifted tone from "feature locking" to "growth partnership"

**Impact:** Establishes positive framing for all commercial interactions

---

### ✅ Amendment 4: Transparent Annual Savings Presentation

**Section Added:** Section 3.3  
**Founder Decision:** Decision #5 (Transparent Annual Savings)

**What Changed:**
- Added required display elements (monthly, regular annual, discounted annual, savings)
- Established standard language: "Pay annually and save 25% — equivalent to 3 free months"
- Mandated consistency across all touchpoints
- Philosophy: Recommended, never forced

**Impact:** Customers never need to calculate savings themselves; builds trust through transparency

---

### ✅ Amendment 5: Enterprise Strategic Partnership Model

**Section Modified:** Section 3.4 (formerly 3.3, renumbered)  
**Founder Decision:** Decision #6 (Strategic Partnership Model)

**What Changed:**
- Complete rewrite from pricing tier to strategic partnership model
- Removed all minimums (monthly amount, annual contract, duration)
- Shifted focus from revenue thresholds to operational complexity
- Established consultation-based engagement (not self-service)
- Clarified Founder approval requirement

**Impact:** Enterprise serves strategic partners of any size based on fit, not arbitrary thresholds

---

### ✅ Amendment 6: Three-Layer Global Commercial Model

**Section Modified:** Section 4 (complete restructure)  
**Founder Decision:** Decision #4 (Global Commercial Model)

**What Changed:**
- Restructured from 2-layer to 3-layer model
- Layer 1: Canonical Commercial Pricing (internal, stable, authoritative)
- Layer 2: Localized Display Pricing (customer-facing, approximate, informational)
- Layer 3: Regional Commercial Policy (future capability, not active in RC1)
- Clarified that architecture supports regional pricing, but policy remains globally consistent

**Impact:** Provides architectural flexibility for future market strategies while maintaining global consistency

---

- Reinforced Commercial Truth compliance

### ✅ Amendment 8: Progressive Commercial Discovery

**Section Modified:** Section 7 (complete rewrite)  
**Founder Decision:** Decision #1 (Progressive Commercial Discovery)

**What Changed:**
- Renamed from "Dashboard Visibility Philosophy" to "Progressive Commercial Discovery"
- Removed "Founder Decision Required" (decision made)
- Established 6 core principles (Show Owned, Expose Next Step, Hide Distant, etc.)
- Defined 4 visibility tiers (Owned, Next-Tier, Distant, Administrative)

- Codified "Administrative Functionality is Never Upgrade Marketing"

**Impact:** Customers never feel restricted—platform grows with their business

### ✅ Amendment 9: Guided Professional Trial
