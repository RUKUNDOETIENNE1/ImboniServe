# PRICING_PAGE_PUNCHLIST

**Date:** 2026-07-02  
**Scope:** Pricing page certification findings only (no implementation yet)

---

## P0 — Must Fix Before Founder Approval

1. **Plan naming/model mismatch in public communication**
   - **Issue:** Public-facing plan naming and descriptors are inconsistent with the approved model framing (Starter → Professional → Business → Premium → Enterprise).
   - **Impact:** High risk of purchase confusion and distrust.
   - **Expected outcome:** one canonical plan ladder and consistent naming across all displayed copy.

2. **Conflicting promotional framing vs premium pricing confidence**
   - **Issue:** “Launch Special: 50% OFF All Plans” is highly prominent and conflicts with premium, stable pricing positioning.
   - **Impact:** Undermines trust and perceived pricing permanence.
   - **Expected outcome:** pricing page leads with clear value and transparent model, not campaign-led urgency.

3. **Global-by-Design inconsistency in pricing copy**
   - **Issue:** country-specific wording remains in pricing language.
   - **Impact:** Contradicts global public positioning and introduces market-scope ambiguity.
   - **Expected outcome:** localization-ready, non-country-assumptive public pricing messaging.

---

## P1 — Strong Improvements (Should Fix for Certification Quality)

1. **Annual value message incomplete**
   - **Issue:** “Save 25%” is shown, but “equivalent to 3 free months” is not explicit where decisions happen.
   - **Impact:** Savings value is under-communicated.

2. **Plan fit clarity (who each plan is for) is too weak**
   - **Issue:** users must infer fit from long feature lists.
   - **Impact:** slower decision, lower confidence.

3. **Upgrade rationale is feature-heavy, outcome-light**
   - **Issue:** cards read as checklists, not business-stage progression.
   - **Impact:** customers may not see why paying more helps growth outcomes.

4. **Messaging tone inconsistency**
   - **Issue:** page mixes premium product language with campaign/jargon-style copy.
   - **Impact:** brand trust and conversion confidence decrease.

5. **Localization polish/consistency**
   - **Issue:** minor translation artifacts and duplicated pricing blocks risk inconsistent output by locale.
   - **Impact:** uneven trust across languages.

---

## P2 — Nice to Improve (Post-Approval Optimization)

1. **Feature list simplification for first pass**
   - Collapse dense bullets into category summaries for faster scanning.

2. **Decision-support comparison cues**
   - Add plain-language “best for” and “when to upgrade” microcopy.

3. **CTA guidance enhancement**
   - Add contextual CTA helper text for owners unsure which plan fits.

4. **Visual de-noising**
   - Reduce badge/icon clutter and prioritize one primary visual signal per card.

---

## Notes

- No pricing model change is requested in this punchlist.
- Scope is presentation, narrative clarity, and trust alignment only.
