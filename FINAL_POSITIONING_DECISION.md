# FINAL POSITIONING DECISION — The 90-Second Test

**Sprint:** Product Differentiation & Positioning Sprint (PDPS)  
**Question:** If a hospitality business spends 90 seconds on our website, what are the 5–10 ideas they should remember?

---

## The 90-Second Memory Test

A hospitality business owner lands on imboniserve.com. They spend 90 seconds scrolling. They leave.

**What do they remember?**

Not 38 capabilities. Not a feature catalogue. Not a pricing table.

They remember **7 ideas**:

---

### Idea 1: "I can replace my POS, my pen-and-paper, and my spreadsheet with one platform."

**Source Capabilities:** QR Code Ordering, KDS, Waiter Dashboard, Inventory, Close Day

**The Memory:** ImboniServe is not a POS add-on. It's not a QR menu app. It's the operating system that runs the entire hospitality business — from customer scan to kitchen to delivery to end-of-day reconciliation.

**Why It Sticks:** The hero says "The Operating System for Hospitality." The carousel shows QR ordering, AI, and intelligence dashboards. The message is clear: one platform, everything covered.

---

### Idea 2: "They have something called Service Replay™ — I've never seen that anywhere."

**Source Capability:** Service Replay™

**The Memory:** A system that lets you replay any service period event-by-event, like watching a football match replay. Every order, every station, every table — with playback controls and speed adjustment.

**Why It Sticks:** The name is memorable. The analogy is intuitive. No competitor offers this. It's the single most unique capability in the platform, and it earns a dedicated homepage section with the headline: "Replay your service like a football match."

---

### Idea 3: "AI actually does work here — it's not just a buzzword."

**Source Capabilities:** AI Menu Builder, Auto-Reorder AI, AI Insight Reports, Cost Anomaly Alerts, A/B Testing

**The Memory:** AI builds your menu from a photo. AI suggests reorders before you run out. AI writes weekly business reports. AI catches supplier price increases. AI tests your menu prices.

**Why It Sticks:** The "Why AI?" section shows four concrete AI capabilities, each with a specific outcome — not "AI-powered insights" but "Upload a photo. Get a menu." The specificity makes it credible.

---

### Idea 4: "They have CFO and CEO dashboards — that's enterprise-level."

**Source Capabilities:** CFO Dashboard, CEO Dashboard

**The Memory:** This isn't just a POS with sales charts. There's a CFO dashboard with financial health scores, revenue intelligence, and AI narratives. There's a CEO dashboard with business health, customer metrics, and operations in one view.

**Why It Sticks:** The "Why Trust Us?" section positions these as proof that ImboniServe is built for decision-makers. A restaurant owner who has never had access to CFO-level intelligence immediately understands the value.

---

### Idea 5: "They know who my customers are — not just how many visited."

**Source Capabilities:** CRM with RFM Segmentation, WhatsApp Campaigns

**The Memory:** Automatic customer segmentation: Champions, Loyal, At Risk, Lost. Lifetime value. Spend analysis. And then you can send targeted WhatsApp campaigns to each segment.

**Why It Sticks:** The "Why Switch?" section contrasts ImboniServe's RFM segmentation with the basic customer lists that most POS systems provide. The message: "Know your customers like an e-commerce brand does."

---

### Idea 6: "I can test my menu prices scientifically — not by guessing."

**Source Capability:** A/B Testing for Menus

**The Memory:** Create price variants, split traffic, measure conversion, pick the winner. This is web-marketing-level optimization applied to restaurant menus.

**Why It Sticks:** "Stop guessing. Start testing." is a headline that resonates with any business owner who has wondered whether they should raise prices. The concept is familiar from e-commerce but unheard of in hospitality POS.

---

### Idea 7: "This platform helps me grow, not just operate."

**Source Capabilities:** Discovery Listing, WhatsApp Campaigns, Site Builder, Marketer Dashboard

**The Memory:** After switching, I get listed on a public directory where customers find me. I can build my own website with AI. I can run referral programs. I can send targeted campaigns.

**Why It Sticks:** The Growth section answers "What happens after I switch?" — the question that every prospect asks but most POS websites ignore. ImboniServe positions itself as a growth partner, not just an operations tool.

## Decision Framework

### What Goes on the Homepage?

A capability earns homepage placement if it answers **yes** to at least one:

| Test | Question |
|------|----------|
| Uniqueness | Would a hospitality business say "I've never seen another system do this"? |
| Problem Severity | Does this solve one of the top 5 operational problems for a hospitality business? |
| Story Necessity | Does removing this capability break the narrative arc? |

### What Goes on Feature Pages?

A capability earns feature page placement if it answers **yes** to:

| Test | Question |
|------|----------|
| Value | Does a customer benefit from knowing this exists? |
| Depth | Does this demonstrate platform depth beyond expectations? |

### What Stays in the Dashboard?

A capability stays dashboard-only if:

| Test | Question |
|------|----------|
| Internal | Is this configuration or settings that customers expect but don't evaluate? |
| Baseline | Is this security, notifications, or organizational structure? |
