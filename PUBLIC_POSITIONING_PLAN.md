# Public Positioning Plan
## ImboniServe — Aligning Public Experience with Full Platform Capabilities

**Date:** 2025
**Objective:** Close the gap between 74 production-ready dashboard capabilities and the current public-facing messaging

---

## 1. Current Positioning Assessment

### Current Public Narrative
> "The Operating System for Hospitality. Run your café, hotel, bar, or hospitality business from one intelligent platform."

### Strengths
- Clear "OS" positioning that implies comprehensiveness
- Strong real-time messaging (live sales, live tables, live orders)
- Founding program creates urgency
- Pricing is transparent and tiered
- Discovery marketplace is well-promoted

### Weaknesses
- Homepage communicates ~65% of actual capabilities
- No dedicated pages for hotel, enterprise, or specific verticals
- FAQ covers only payment fees, not product capabilities
- "30+ features" stat undersells (actual: 50+ distinct capabilities across 74 pages)
- No screenshots or interactive demos of key dashboards
- CFO/CEO/Marketer dashboards — major enterprise selling points — are invisible
- Site builder is invisible (eliminates Wix/Squarespace cost)
- Recipe management is invisible (underpins "accurate food costs" claim)

## 6. Messaging Hierarchy by Audience

### For Small Cafés / Food Stalls (Starter Plan)
**Lead with:** QR ordering, menu builder, mobile money, basic inventory
**Support with:** Discovery listing, referrals, daily reports
**Don't overwhelm with:** Enterprise features (CFO, multi-branch, KDS advanced)

### For Established Restaurants (Professional Plan)
**Lead with:** KDS, reservations, inventory alerts, staff management, payment analytics
**Support with:** CRM, campaigns, menu performance, peak hours, recipe management
**Show path to:** Multi-branch, advanced analytics

### For Hotels & Chains (Business/Premium Plan)
**Lead with:** Multi-branch, KDS, hotel management, CFO dashboard, payout reconciliation
**Support with:** Site builder, CMS, advanced reporting, staff performance
**Show path to:** Enterprise (custom roles, SLA, dedicated support)

### For Large Organizations (Enterprise Plan)
**Lead with:** CEO dashboard, custom roles, audit exports, SLA, dedicated account manager
**Support with:** Everything in Premium
**CTA:** Contact sales (WhatsApp) — not self-serve signup
