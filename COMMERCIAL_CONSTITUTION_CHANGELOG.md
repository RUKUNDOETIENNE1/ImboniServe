## VERSION 1.1 AMENDMENTS (2026-07-03)

**Approved By:** Founder  
**Amendment Date:** 2026-07-03  
**Reason:** Incorporate six strategic Founder decisions from commercial review

### AMENDMENT 1: Product Demonstration Principle

**Section Added:** Section 1.5 — Product Demonstration Principle

**Change Type:** New Section

**Content Added:**
- Distinction between Public Website, Product Demonstration, Guided Trial, and Commercial Subscription
- Demonstration philosophy: Educational experiences, not commercial entitlements
- Clarification that demonstrations may showcase any capability without implying subscription inclusion
- Commercial access governed only by purchased subscriptions

**Rationale:**  
Establishes clear separation between sales demonstrations (educational) and commercial entitlements (contractual). Prevents confusion about what customers are entitled to based on what they've seen in demos.

**Founder Decision:** Decision #3 (Guided Professional Trial) — Product Demonstration Principle component

---

### AMENDMENT 2: "Only Sell What Exists" Philosophy

**Section Modified:** Principle 2 (Section 1.2)

**Change Type:** Content Enhancement

**Before:**
> "No feature may be advertised on the pricing page, marketing materials, or sales conversations unless it exists and functions as described."

**After:**
> "The pricing page is a commercial contract, not a product roadmap. Only production-ready capabilities may be advertised in commercial packaging. Future capabilities belong in roadmaps, release notes, and product announcements—never in pricing materials. If a capability is usable but still evolving, it may be labeled 'Early Access.' If a capability is not operational, it must be removed from pricing until ready."

**Rationale:**  
Strengthens Commercial Truth by explicitly prohibiting "roadmap as pricing" and establishing clear guidelines for production-ready vs. evolving vs. future capabilities.

**Founder Decision:** Decision #2 (Only Sell What Exists)

---

### AMENDMENT 3: Customer Growth Philosophy

**Section Modified:** Principle 5 (Section 1.2)

**Change Type:** Content Enhancement

**Before:**
> "Customers must clearly understand what they're purchasing, what they're receiving, and how to access more value. Confusion is a commercial failure."

**After:**
> "Customers must clearly understand what they're purchasing, what they're receiving, and how to access more value. Confusion is a commercial failure. Customers should never feel restricted—they should feel that the platform grows together with their business."

**Rationale:**  
Establishes positive framing for commercial discovery. Shifts from "feature locking" to "growth partnership."

**Founder Decision:** Decision #1 (Progressive Commercial Discovery)

### AMENDMENT 4: Expanded Anti-Patterns

**Section Modified:** Section 1.3 — Anti-Patterns

**Change Type:** Content Addition

- ❌ **Restrictive UX** — Making customers feel limited rather than supported in their growth journey

Codifies prohibited practices identified during Founder review.

**Founder Decision:** Decision #2 (Only Sell What Exists) and Decision #1 (Progressive Commercial Discovery)

### AMENDMENT 5: Transparent Annual Savings Presentation

**Section Added:** Section 3.3 — Annual Billing Presentation

**Change Type:** New Section

**Content Added:**
- Required display elements (monthly price, regular annual, discounted annual, savings)
- Standard language: "Pay annually and save 25% — equivalent to 3 free months."
- Consistency requirements across all touchpoints
- Philosophy: Recommended, never forced

**Rationale:**  
Establishes clear, consistent presentation of annual billing value. Prevents customers from needing to calculate savings themselves.

**Founder Decision:** Decision #5 (Transparent Annual Savings)

### AMENDMENT 6: Enterprise Strategic Partnership Model

**Section Modified:** Section 3.3 → Section 3.4 (renumbered)

**Change Type:** Complete Rewrite

**Before:**
> "Enterprise pricing is custom and negotiated based on: [list of factors]. Enterprise contracts are individually approved by the Founder."

**After:**
> "Enterprise is not simply another subscription tier. Enterprise represents a strategic partnership that begins where the standard platform ends. [Detailed partnership model with no minimums, operational complexity focus, consultation-based engagement]"

**Key Changes:**
- Removed all minimum requirements (monthly amount, annual contract, duration)
- Shifted focus from revenue thresholds to operational complexity
- Established consultation-based engagement model (not self-service)
- Clarified Founder approval requirement

**Rationale:**  
Reframes Enterprise as strategic partnership rather than "highest tier." Allows flexibility to serve strategic partners of any size based on fit, not arbitrary minimums.

**Founder Decision:** Decision #6 (Strategic Partnership Model)

### AMENDMENT 8: Production-Ready Feature Requirements

**Section Modified:** Section 6.7 — Feature Status Notes → Feature Status and Commercial Packaging

**Change Type:** Complete Rewrite

**Before:**
> "Fully Functional: Most features listed above are fully functional and production-ready. Partial Implementation: [list of 5 features]. Founder Decision Required: Should these features be: 1) Completed before RC1 launch? 2) Removed from pricing page until complete? 3) Marked as 'Coming Soon' on pricing page?"

**After:**
> "Production-Ready Features: All features listed in this section (6.2-6.6) are production-ready and may be included in commercial packaging. Features Under Development: Features that are not yet production-ready must not appear in commercial packaging. Early Access Features: If a capability is usable but still evolving, it may be included with an 'Early Access' label. Commercial Truth Compliance: Only production-ready capabilities may be advertised in commercial packaging."

**Key Changes:**
- Removed "Founder Decision Required" (decision made)
- Removed list of partial implementations (no longer relevant)
- Established clear production-ready requirement
- Defined "Early Access" labeling option
- Reinforced Commercial Truth compliance

**Rationale:**  
Implements "Only Sell What Exists" philosophy. Removes ambiguity about what can be included in commercial packaging.

**Founder Decision:** Decision #2 (Only Sell What Exists)

### AMENDMENT 9: Progressive Commercial Discovery

**Section Modified:** Section 7 — Dashboard Visibility Philosophy → Progressive Commercial Discovery

**Change Type:** Complete Rewrite

**Before:**
- 7.1 Visibility Principles (4 principles)
- 7.2 Visibility Tiers (3 tiers with "Founder Decision Required")
- 7.3 Upgrade Prompts

**After:**

Implements Progressive Commercial Discovery philosophy. Customers never feel restricted—they feel supported in their growth journey.

**Founder Decision:** Decision #1 (Progressive Commercial Discovery)

### AMENDMENT 10: Guided Professional Trial

**Section Modified:** Section 8 — Trial Policy → Guided Professional Trial

**Change Type:** Significant Enhancement

**Before:**
- 8.1 Trial Philosophy
- 8.2 Recommended Trial Strategy (Professional features)
- 8.3 Trial Eligibility
- 8.4 Trial Conversion Flow
- 8.5 Trial Expiry Behavior

**After:**
- 8.1 Trial Philosophy (enhanced: guided learning journey)
- 8.2 Trial Strategy (Professional features with progressive introduction)
- 8.3 Trial Eligibility (unchanged)
- 8.4 Trial Conversion Flow (enhanced: usage-based recommendations)
- 8.5 Trial Expiry Behavior (unchanged)

**Key Changes:**
- Reframed trial as "guided learning journey" not just "feature access"
- Added progressive introduction timeline (Days 1-3, 4-7, 8-11, 12-14)
- Changed conversion flow to usage-based recommendations
- At expiry, recommend plan based on actual usage (not all plans)
- Objective: Build operational confidence progressively, not overwhelm on Day One

**Rationale:**  
Transforms trial from passive feature access to active guided learning. Ensures customers understand value progressively and subscribe to the plan that best fits their actual needs.

**Founder Decision:** Decision #3 (Guided Professional Trial)
