# HOMEPAGE PRIORITY — What Deserves Homepage Prominence

**Sprint:** Product Differentiation & Positioning Sprint (PDPS)  
**Principle:** Homepage space is earned through customer impact, not technical capability

---

## Homepage Principle

> Do not build a feature catalogue. Build a story.

Every homepage section must answer one of five questions:

| Question | Answered By |
|----------|------------|
| **Why switch?** | Capabilities that solve problems competitors ignore |
| **Why trust us?** | Capabilities that demonstrate depth and intelligence |
| **Why now?** | Capabilities that create urgency — the cost of waiting |
| **Why AI?** | Capabilities where AI creates unfair advantage |
| **Why ImboniServe?** | The unique combination that no competitor offers |

---

## Recommended Homepage Structure

## Homepage Word Count Budget

| Section | Estimated Words | Purpose |
|---------|----------------|---------|
| Hero | 50 | Hook — "The Operating System for Hospitality" |
| Why Switch? | 150 | Three differentiators with one-sentence proof each |
| Why AI? | 150 | Four AI capabilities with one-sentence outcome each |
| Why Trust Us? | 100 | Two executive dashboards as proof of depth |
| Why Now? | 75 | Five urgency lines connecting pain to solution |
| Growth | 100 | Three growth capabilities |
| Social Proof + Pricing | 50 | Trust signals and CTAs |
| **Total** | **~675 words** | |

A visitor spending 90 seconds reads approximately 300-400 words. The most important sections (Hero, Why Switch?, Why AI?) total ~350 words — exactly within the 90-second attention window.
