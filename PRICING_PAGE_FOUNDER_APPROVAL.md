## Rationale

The Pricing page is close to approval, but current communication quality does not yet match the finalized Homepage standard.

### Evidence for this recommendation

1. **Core strengths are present**
   - Tiered plans are visible and broadly understandable.
   - Monthly/annual toggle exists and shows annual savings.
   - Currency configuration capability supports Global-by-Design direction.

2. **Minor-but-important trust gaps remain**
   - Public plan naming/presentation is not yet fully aligned to the approved commercial ladder language.
   - Promotional framing (“launch special”) is too dominant for a pricing page that should maximize confidence and clarity.
   - Country-specific wording still appears in pricing messaging, weakening global public positioning.

3. **Decision support can be clearer**
   - “Who this plan is for” and “why upgrade” are not yet obvious enough for first-time restaurant owners scanning quickly.
   - “Save 25% = 3 free months” equivalence is not explicit at key decision points.
